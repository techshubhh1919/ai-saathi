01. The Ground Reality

Why Rural Citizens Are Excluded from Digital India

🗣️

Language & Literacy

Government portals use bureaucratic, complex language. Farmers and village women cannot navigate English or text-heavy forms.

💸

The "Dalal" Tax

Villagers travel 10–15 km to town cyber cafes, paying ₹200–₹500 fees to middle-men just to fill simple welfare applications.

🚨

Rural Cyber Scams

Fake electricity disconnection SMS and lottery links trick first-time smartphone users into downloading APKs, losing savings.

02. The Solution

AI Saathi: 4 Pillars of Rural Empowerment

🎙️

Voice-First Vernacular Interaction

Speak naturally in Hindi/regional dialects. Audio answers read aloud so anyone can listen.

🌾

Instant Eligibility Matching

3 quick questions match farmers, students, and women to exact schemes (PM-Kisan, Ayushman, PMAY).

📄

Document OCR & Missing Info

Camera photo of Aadhaar/Ration card immediately warns if mobile linkage or details are missing.

🛡️

Cyber Scam Shield

Real-time scam classification with clear voice alert and national helpline 1930 integration.

03. Technical Architecture

Resilient, Low-Bandwidth & Multimodal Tech Stack

Frontend Layer

Web Speech API

Zero-latency browser STT & TTS in Hindi (hi-IN) without requiring expensive external voice API tokens.

Backend & AI Brain

FastAPI + Gemini Flash

High-speed asynchronous Python backend with Gemini intelligence and seamless offline heuristic fallbacks.

Omnichannel Reach

WhatsApp Bot & PWA

Twilio-compatible WhatsApp integration allows users on basic smartphones to access services without installing apps.
