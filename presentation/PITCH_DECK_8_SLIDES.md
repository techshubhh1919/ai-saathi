## Slide 2: The Ground Reality (Problem Statement)
1. **The Literacy & Language Barrier**: Most government schemes (PM-Kisan, Ayushman Bharat, Scholarships) have portals in complex bureaucratic Hindi or English.
2. **The "Cyber Cafe / Dalal" Tax**: Villagers pay ₹100–₹500 to middle-men just to fill simple forms or check eligibility.
3. **Exploding Rural Cyber Fraud**: Fake electricity bill disconnection SMS and fake lottery messages trick gullible villagers into downloading malicious APKs and losing their life savings.

---

## Slide 3: The Solution — AI Saathi
- **No Typing Needed (Voice-First)**: Speak naturally in Hindi, Bhojpuri, or local dialect.
- **Intelligent Schemes Finder**: Conversational questionnaire matches exactly which schemes you are eligible for in seconds.
- **Document OCR Inspector**: Camera scan of Aadhaar / Ration card immediately warns about missing mobile links or mismatched details.
- **Instant Cyber Fraud Shield**: Paste or read out any suspicious SMS/WhatsApp message — AI Saathi flags scams with 98%+ confidence and speaks out safety steps.

---

## Slide 4: Architecture & Innovation
- **Voice In/Out**: Low-latency Web Speech API (`SpeechRecognition` & `SpeechSynthesis`) tailored for Indian phonetics.
- **AI Core**: FastAPI + Gemini Multimodal Intelligence for colloquial Indian language comprehension and fraud pattern recognition.
- **Edge Resilience**: Works on low-bandwidth / 2G networks with client-side cached emergency helplines (1551, 1930, 1090) and scheme criteria.
- **Omnichannel Reach**: Web app for Gram Panchayats + WhatsApp Bot (Twilio) for direct mobile messaging.
