# AI Saathi — 3-Minute Hackathon Demo Rehearsal Script

> **Total Time**: 3 Minutes (180 seconds)  
> **Speaker Roles**:  
> - **Person A**: Tech / AI Architecture & Live Action Driver  
> - **Person B**: Pitch Anchor & Civic Context Narrator  

---

### [0:00 - 0:30] The Hook & Mission (Person B)
- **Person B (Confidently to Judges)**:
  "Good evening, respected judges. In rural India, when a farmer or a village mother gets a government SMS or wants to apply for PM-Kisan, their only option is to travel 10 kilometers to a cyber cafe, pay ₹200 to a middle-man, or fall victim to a fake electricity bill scam.
  We built **AI Saathi (एआई साथी)** — India's first truly accessible, voice-first rural civic assistant."

---

### [0:30 - 1:15] Voice-First Scheme Eligibility (Person A & System)
- **Person A (Interacting with UI)**:
  "Watch how simple this is. No typing, no English jargon. Let's tap the mic."
  *(Person A taps the big amber mic button on screen)*
- **Person A speaks in Hindi**:
  *"मुझे पीएम किसान और आयुष्मान कार्ड का फायदा कैसे मिलेगा?"*
- **System Audio speaks out**:
  *"पीएम किसान में सालाना ₹6,000 और आयुष्मान भारत में ₹5 लाख तक का मुफ्त इलाज मिलता है। आपकी पात्रता के अनुसार 2 योजनाएं स्वीकृत हैं।"*
- **Person A**:
  "Notice how the UI visually breaks down the exact required documents in simple Devanagari and gives audible instructions for elderly citizens who cannot read."

---

### [1:15 - 2:00] Document Scanner & Missing Info Check (Person A)
- **Person A**:
  "Now, let's look at our Document OCR Inspector. Often villagers' applications get rejected after 3 months because of one missing detail. Let's scan an Aadhaar card."
  *(Person A clicks 'Simulate Scan')*
- **Person A**:
  "Look at what happened in less than 1 second: AI Saathi extracted Rameshwar Prasad's name and village, but immediately flagged a **HIGH SEVERITY WARNING**: 'Mobile number is not linked with Aadhaar'. It speaks aloud the exact solution: visit the nearest post office or CSC center."

---

### [2:00 - 2:40] Real-time Cyber Scam Shield (Person B)
- **Person B**:
  "Now the biggest threat facing rural India: WhatsApp & SMS cyber fraud. Every day, farmers receive: *'Electricity will be disconnected tonight, call officer immediately'*. Let's test this message."
  *(Person B clicks 'बिजली बिल कटने का फर्जी SMS')*
- **Person B**:
  "Instant **HIGH DANGER 98% Alert**! It clearly explains why this is a scam, forbids clicking links, and provides the direct National Cyber Crime Helpline **1930**."

---

### [2:40 - 3:00] Conclusion & Why We Win (Person B & Person A)
- **Person B**:
  "AI Saathi works on 2G connections, operates via Web and WhatsApp, and brings genuine Digital Inclusion to 80 crore rural citizens."
- **Person A**:
  "Thank you, judges! We are happy to take your questions."
